Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This policy explains what AyuAI processes and stores. We aim to keep data collection limited and transparent.

Information we collect

When you use AyuAI, the service receives the messages, conversation history, mode, and optional browser memory that you send to generate a response. We do not require an account.

What we store

Chats and the optional name memory are stored in your browser's sessionStorage. This is temporary browser storage and is normally cleared when the browser tab or session ends. AyuAI does not currently store chat history in its own database.

Email, name, and account information

We do not collect email addresses, names, or account information through an account system. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include in that email.

Third-party processing

To generate responses, message content may be sent to Pollinations, Groq, and OpenRouter through their APIs. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. AyuAI does not sell your information or use advertising cookies.

Retention and deletion

Browser-stored chats remain until the session ends, you delete them, or your browser removes site data. Use “Delete chat” or “Delete all” in the sidebar to remove local conversations. For privacy questions or a deletion request concerning information you emailed us, contact ayuxd85@gmail.com.

Contact

For privacy questions, write to ayuxd85@gmail.com.
